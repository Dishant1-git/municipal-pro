MUNICIPAL SERVICES PORTAL - OFFICIAL KNOWLEDGE BASE & FAQ

--- 1. GENERAL INFORMATION ---
What is the Municipal Services Portal?
The Municipal Services Portal is a unified platform for citizens to report local issues, request municipal services, and track the progress of their complaints online.

Who can use this portal?
Any citizen can sign up and use the portal to report issues in their locality. Municipal workers and administrators also use the portal to manage and resolve these issues.

--- 2. ACCOUNT & REGISTRATION ---
How do I create an account?
Click on the "Sign Up" button on the homepage. You will need to provide your Name, Email Address, and create a Password. Once registered, you can log in immediately.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Currently, password resets are handled manually. Please contact our support team via the "Contact" page or email us directly.

Is my data secure?
Yes. All passwords are encrypted securely. We do not share your personal information with unauthorized third parties.

--- 3. FILING A COMPLAINT ---
How do I file a new complaint?
1. Log in to your account.
2. Click on "File New Complaint" or navigate to the "Post Complaint" section.
3. Fill in the required details: Your Name, Email, Phone number, Problem Category, Address of the issue, and detailed description.
4. You MUST upload an image (picture) of the problem to help our workers identify it.
5. Click Submit.

What types of problems can I report?
You can report various municipal issues including:
- Street light outages
- Water supply issues or leaks
- Garbage collection and sanitation problems
- Potholes and road damage
- Public park maintenance
- Drainage and sewage issues

Do I need to upload a photo?
Yes, a photograph of the issue is mandatory when filing a complaint. This helps our workers locate and assess the severity of the problem quickly.

--- 4. COMPLAINT TRACKING & STATUSES ---
How do I check the status of my complaint?
Navigate to "My Complaints" from your user dashboard. You will see a list of all your reported issues and their current statuses.

What do the different complaint statuses mean?
- Processed: Your complaint has been received and is waiting to be assigned to a worker by the admin.
- Assigned to worker: An administrator has assigned your complaint to a specific municipal worker with a priority level (High, Medium, Low).
- Completed: The assigned worker has resolved the issue.
- Revert to admin: The worker could not complete the task (e.g., due to wrong address or requiring different equipment) and has sent it back to the admin with a reason.

Will I be notified about my complaint?
Yes, our system sends automated email notifications to keep you informed when your complaint is received and being processed.

--- 5. WORKER & ADMIN PROCESSES (How it works behind the scenes) ---
Who fixes the problems?
Our registered municipal workers handle the physical resolution of complaints. 

How does the process work?
1. You submit a complaint.
2. The Administrator reviews it on the Admin Dashboard and assigns it to the appropriate worker, setting a Priority (High, Medium, or Low) and adding specific instructions.
3. The Worker sees the task on their "Worker Dashboard".
4. The Worker goes to the location, fixes the issue, and updates the status to "Completed" on the portal.

What happens if a worker cannot fix the issue?
If a worker cannot resolve an issue, they will use the "Revert to admin" option and provide a detailed reason (Addon message). The Admin will then review the unassigned or reverted tasks and take appropriate alternative action.

--- 6. SERVICES ---
What other services do you offer?
You can view all our available services on the "Services" page. This may include information about property tax, water connection applications, and waste management schedules.

How to apply for a water connection?
You can apply online through the citizen portal or visit the local municipal office. Required documents typically include ID proof, address proof, and property tax receipt.

How to pay property tax online?
Property tax details can be found under the "Services" tab. Online payments are coming soon.
